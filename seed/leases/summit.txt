                     COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
                           EXCERPT

                    CASCADE TOWER — SUITE 410

LANDLORD:    Cascade Property Holdings LLC

                        ARTICLE I — PREMISES

Section 1.1  Premises.  Landlord leases to Tenant approximately
six thousand two hundred (6,200) rentable square feet located
at Suite 410, Floor 4, Cascade Tower, 500 Commerce Drive,
Chicago, IL 60601 (the "Premises").

Section 1.2  Building.  The Building contains approximately
fifty thousand (50,000) rentable square feet.

                     ARTICLE II — LEASE TERM

Section 2.1  Term.  The Lease commences on January 1, 2021 and
expires on December 31, 2026 (the "Term").

                      ARTICLE III — BASE RENT

Section 3.1  Monthly Base Rent.  Tenant shall pay monthly base
rent of Fifteen Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($15,500.00) per
month, subject to annual escalation.

Section 3.2  Annual Escalation.  Base Rent shall increase by
three and one-half percent (3.5%) per annum on each anniversary
of the Commencement Date.

                 ARTICLE IV — OPERATING EXPENSES

Section 4.1  Gross Lease.  This is a gross lease. Tenant is not
separately responsible for any portion of Operating Expenses.
All operating costs, including real estate taxes, insurance,
and common area maintenance, are included in the Monthly Base
Rent.

                    ARTICLE V — LEASE TYPE

Section 5.1  Gross Lease.  This Lease is a full-service gross
lease.

                  ARTICLE VI — MISCELLANEOUS

Section 6.1  Parking.  Tenant is entitled to twelve (12) parking
spaces in the Cascade Tower parking garage at no additional cost.

Section 6.2  Signage.  Tenant shall have the right to install
identification signage on the suite entry door and in the main
lobby directory, subject to Landlord's reasonable design approval.

Section 6.3  HVAC Hours.  Standard HVAC service: Monday through
Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM; Saturday, 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
After-hours HVAC available at $75.00 per hour.

Section 6.4  Renewal Option.  Tenant has one (1) option to renew
for an additional five (5) years at then-prevailing market rates,
exercisable by written notice no later than nine (9) months prior
to the expiration of the Term.
