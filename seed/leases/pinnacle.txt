                        ARTICLE I — PREMISES

Section 1.1  Premises.  Landlord leases to Tenant approximately
six thousand two hundred (6,200) rentable square feet located
at Suite 502, Floor 5, Cascade Tower, 500 Commerce Drive,
Chicago, IL 60601 (the "Premises").

Section 1.2  Building.  The Building contains approximately
fifty thousand (50,000) rentable square feet.

                     ARTICLE II — LEASE TERM

Section 2.1  Term.  The Lease commences on January 1, 2020 and
expires on December 31, 2029 (the "Term").

                      ARTICLE III — BASE RENT

Section 3.1  Monthly Base Rent.  Tenant shall pay monthly base
rent of Eleven Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($11,500.00) per
month, subject to annual escalation.

Section 3.2  Annual Escalation.  Base Rent shall increase by
three percent (3.0%) per annum on each anniversary of the
Commencement Date.

                 ARTICLE IV — OPERATING EXPENSES

Section 4.1  Base Year.  Calendar year 2020. Base operating
expense: $11.60 per rentable square foot.

Section 4.2  Current Year Operating Expense.  Calendar year
2024: $13.40 per rentable square foot.

Section 4.3  Tenant's Pro-Rata Share.
  Tenant's Share = 6,200 / 50,000 = 12.40%
  Pro-rata denominator is the total building area (50,000 RSF).

Section 4.4  Operating Expense Cap.
  Controllable operating expenses are capped at six percent (6%)
  per annum on a cumulative basis. Non-controllable expenses (taxes,
  insurance, utilities) are excluded from the cap.

                     ARTICLE V — MISCELLANEOUS

Section 5.1  Utilities and Services.  Landlord shall provide HVAC
during standard business hours (8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through
Friday). After-hours HVAC is billed at $75.00 per hour.
