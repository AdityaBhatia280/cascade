                        ARTICLE I — PREMISES

Section 1.1  Premises.  Landlord leases to Tenant approximately
ten thousand five hundred (10,500) rentable square feet located
at Unit G01, Ground Floor, Cascade Industrial Park, Building 2,
Chicago, IL 60601 (the "Premises").

Section 1.2  Building.  The Building contains approximately
forty thousand (40,000) rentable square feet.

                     ARTICLE II — LEASE TERM

Section 2.1  Term.  The Lease commences on May 1, 2021 and
expires on April 30, 2026 (the "Term").

                      ARTICLE III — BASE RENT

Section 3.1  Monthly Base Rent.  Tenant shall pay monthly base
rent of Fourteen Thousand Dollars ($14,000.00) per month, subject
to annual escalation.

Section 3.2  Annual Escalation.  Base Rent shall increase by
two percent (2.0%) per annum on each anniversary of the
Commencement Date.

                 ARTICLE IV — OPERATING EXPENSES

Section 4.1  Base Year.  Calendar year 2021. Base operating
expense: $7.50 per rentable square foot.

Section 4.2  Current Year Operating Expense.  Calendar year
2024: $8.90 per rentable square foot.

Section 4.3  Tenant's Pro-Rata Share.
  Tenant's Share = 10,500 / 40,000 = 26.25%
  Pro-rata denominator is the total building area (40,000 RSF).

Section 4.4  Triple Net Lease (NNN).
  Tenant pays pro-rata share of all operating costs, taxes, and
  insurance without cap.
