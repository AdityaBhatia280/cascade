                        ARTICLE I — PREMISES

Section 1.1  Premises.  Landlord leases to Tenant approximately
four thousand eight hundred (4,800) rentable square feet located
at Suite 305, Floor 3, Cascade Tower, 500 Commerce Drive,
Chicago, IL 60601 (the "Premises").

Section 1.2  Building.  The Building contains approximately
fifty thousand (50,000) rentable square feet.

                     ARTICLE II — LEASE TERM

Section 2.1  Term.  The Lease commences on July 1, 2022 and
expires on June 30, 2027 (the "Term").

                      ARTICLE III — BASE RENT

Section 3.1  Monthly Base Rent.  Tenant shall pay monthly base
rent of Eight Thousand Nine Hundred Dollars ($8,900.00) per
month, subject to annual escalation.

Section 3.2  Annual Escalation.  Base Rent shall increase by
two and one-half percent (2.5%) per annum on each anniversary
of the Commencement Date.

                 ARTICLE IV — OPERATING EXPENSES

Section 4.1  Base Year.  Calendar year 2022. Base operating
expense: $12.80 per rentable square foot.

Section 4.2  Current Year Operating Expense.  Calendar year
2024: $13.40 per rentable square foot.

Section 4.3  Tenant's Pro-Rata Share.

  Tenant's Share = 4,800 / 50,000 = 9.60%

  Pro-rata denominator is the total building area (50,000 RSF).

Section 4.4  Monthly CAM Charge Calculation.

  Expense Increase = $13.40 - $12.80 = $0.60/SF
  Annual CAM = $0.60 × 4,800 = $2,880.00
  Monthly CAM = $2,880.00 ÷ 12 = $240.00

Section 4.5  CAM Cap.  Non-cumulative cap of four percent (4%)
per annum. The year-over-year increase in operating expenses
shall not exceed 4% of the prior year's operating expense level
for purposes of computing Tenant's share.

  2023 cap: $12.80 × 1.04 = $13.312 (actual $13.10 — under cap)
  2024 cap: $13.10 × 1.04 = $13.624 (actual $13.40 — under cap)

                    ARTICLE V — LEASE TYPE

Section 5.1  Modified Gross Lease.  This Lease is a modified
gross lease.

                 ARTICLE VI — MISCELLANEOUS

Section 6.1  Audit Rights.  Tenant shall have the right to
audit Landlord's Operating Expense records within ninety (90)
days following delivery of the annual statement.

Section 6.2  HVAC Hours.  Standard HVAC service: Monday through
Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM; Saturday, 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
After-hours HVAC available at $75.00 per hour.
