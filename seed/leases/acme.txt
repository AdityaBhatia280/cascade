                        ARTICLE I — PREMISES

Section 1.1  Premises.  Landlord leases to Tenant approximately
twelve thousand five hundred (12,500) rentable square feet
comprising the entirety of Data Center Hall 200, Floor 2, Cascade
Tower, 500 Commerce Drive, Chicago, IL 60601 (the "Premises").

Section 1.2  Data Center Classification.  The Premises are
designated as a Tier III data center environment with the
following committed infrastructure:

  (a) N+1 cooling redundancy (minimum 2 of 3 chillers operational)
  (b) 2N power distribution via UPS-A and UPS-B panels
  (c) Dedicated CRAH units (CRAH-1 through CRAH-4)
  (d) Mantrap physical access control with biometric readers
  (e) Environmental monitoring (temperature, humidity)

                     ARTICLE II — LEASE TERM

Section 2.1  Term.  The Lease commences on January 1, 2020 and
expires on December 31, 2030 (the "Term").

                      ARTICLE III — RENT

Section 3.1  Monthly Fee.  Tenant shall pay a monthly fee of
Forty-Five Thousand Dollars ($45,000.00) per month (the "Monthly
Fee"), inclusive of base rent, power, and cooling allocations.

Section 3.2  No Escalation.  The Monthly Fee is fixed for the
Term and is not subject to annual escalation.

                ARTICLE IV — OPERATING EXPENSES

Section 4.1  Triple Net.  This is a triple net lease (NNN).
Tenant is responsible for its pro-rata share of Operating
Expenses computed using the total building area (50,000 RSF)
as the denominator.

Section 4.2  Base Year.  Calendar year 2020. Base operating
expense: $18.50 per rentable square foot.

Section 4.3  Current Year.  Current operating expense:
$22.10 per rentable square foot (2024).

             ARTICLE V — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

Section 5.1  Cooling SLA.  Landlord guarantees cooling system
availability of ninety-nine and ninety-nine hundredths percent
(99.99%) measured on a monthly basis (the "Cooling SLA"). The
Cooling SLA is measured based on the availability of sufficient
chiller capacity to maintain data center environmental conditions
within ASHRAE A1 guidelines (inlet temperature 18°C–27°C).

Section 5.2  Cooling SLA Measurement.  A "Cooling Failure Event"
occurs when fewer than two (2) of the three (3) building chillers
(CH-1, CH-2, CH-3) are operational simultaneously, as reported
by the Building Management System (BMS). The BMS shall export
a daily status file (COOLFLAG.DAT) indicating cooling status
for each monitored unit.

Section 5.3  SLA Credit Schedule.  In the event of a Cooling
Failure Event, Tenant shall receive a credit against the Monthly
Fee according to the following schedule:

  Duration of Outage          Credit (% of Monthly Fee)
  ─────────────────────────   ─────────────────────────
  Up to 1 hour (0-60 min)     5%  ($2,250.00)
  1-4 hours (61-240 min)     10%  ($4,500.00)
  4-8 hours (241-480 min)    20%  ($9,000.00)
  Over 8 hours (480+ min)    30%  ($13,500.00)

  Credits are non-cumulative within a single calendar month;
  the maximum credit in any month shall be thirty percent (30%)
  of the Monthly Fee.

Section 5.4  SLA Credit Processing.  SLA credits shall be
computed automatically by Landlord's billing system using
cooling status data from the BMS. Credits shall be applied
to the following month's invoice.

Section 5.5  Exclusions.  The Cooling SLA does not apply during:
  (a) Scheduled maintenance windows (with 72 hours prior notice)
  (b) Force majeure events
  (c) Tenant-caused incidents

                ARTICLE VI — PHYSICAL SECURITY

Section 6.1  Access Control.  The Premises shall be secured by
mantrap access control with dual-factor authentication (badge +
biometric). Access is restricted to Tenant-authorized personnel
and pre-approved Landlord maintenance staff.

Section 6.2  Vendor Access.  Vendor access to the Premises
requires written Tenant approval and shall be limited to the
scope and duration of the applicable work order.

                  ARTICLE VII — ENVIRONMENTAL

Section 7.1  Temperature.  Landlord shall maintain cold-aisle
inlet temperatures between 18°C and 27°C per ASHRAE A1 guidelines.

Section 7.2  Humidity.  Landlord shall maintain relative humidity
between 30% and 70% per ASHRAE A1 guidelines.

                 ARTICLE VIII — MISCELLANEOUS

Section 8.1  Infrastructure Dependencies.  Tenant acknowledges
that the data center cooling infrastructure has the following
power dependencies:

  - Chillers CH-1, CH-2, CH-3 are powered via building UPS panels
  - CRAH units CRAH-1 through CRAH-4 are powered via building UPS panels
  - BMS monitoring and access control systems share network infrastructure

Section 8.2  Reporting.  Landlord shall provide monthly uptime
reports including chiller availability, temperature excursions,
and any SLA credit calculations.
