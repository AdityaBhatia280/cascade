                        ARTICLE I — PREMISES

Section 1.1  Premises.  Landlord leases to Tenant approximately
eight thousand two hundred (8,200) rentable square feet located
at Suite 110, Floor 1, Cascade Tower, 500 Commerce Drive,
Chicago, IL 60601 (the "Premises").

Section 1.2  Building.  The Building contains approximately
fifty thousand (50,000) rentable square feet.

                     ARTICLE II — LEASE TERM

Section 2.1  Original Term.  The original Lease commenced on
March 1, 2019 and expires on February 28, 2029 (the "Term").

Section 2.2  Amendment Date.  This First Amendment is effective
as of June 15, 2023.

                      ARTICLE III — BASE RENT

Section 3.1  Monthly Base Rent.  Tenant shall pay monthly base
rent of Fourteen Thousand Three Hundred Fifty Dollars ($14,350.00)
per month, subject to annual escalation as set forth below.

Section 3.2  Annual Escalation.  Base Rent shall increase by
three percent (3%) per annum on each anniversary of the
Commencement Date.

                 ARTICLE IV — OPERATING EXPENSES

Section 4.1  Base Year.  The Base Year for purposes of computing
Tenant's share of Operating Expense increases shall be calendar
year 2019 (the "Original Base Year").

  ** FIRST AMENDMENT — Section 4.1A (replaces Section 4.1): **

  Effective as of the date of this First Amendment (June 15, 2023),
  the Base Year for purposes of computing Tenant's share of
  Operating Expense increases is hereby amended to calendar year
  2021 (the "Amended Base Year"). All references to "Base Year"
  in the Lease shall hereafter refer to calendar year 2021.

  The parties acknowledge the following historical Operating
  Expense levels:

    Calendar Year 2019:  $11.20 per rentable square foot
    Calendar Year 2020:  $11.55 per rentable square foot
    Calendar Year 2021:  $12.05 per rentable square foot (Amended Base)
    Calendar Year 2022:  $12.70 per rentable square foot
    Calendar Year 2023:  $13.10 per rentable square foot
    Calendar Year 2024:  $13.40 per rentable square foot (Current)

Section 4.2  Tenant's Pro-Rata Share.  Tenant's pro-rata share
of Operating Expense increases above the Base Year shall be
computed as follows:

  Tenant's Share = Tenant RSF / Building Total RSF
                 = 8,200 / 50,000
                 = 16.40%

  The pro-rata denominator shall be the total rentable area of
  the Building (50,000 RSF), regardless of occupancy.

Section 4.3  Monthly CAM Charge Calculation.

  Monthly CAM = (Current OpEx/SF - Base Year OpEx/SF)
                × Tenant RSF ÷ 12

  Using Amended Base Year 2021 ($12.05/SF) and current year
  2024 ($13.40/SF):

    Expense Increase = $13.40 - $12.05 = $1.35/SF
    Annual CAM = $1.35 × 8,200 = $11,070.00
    Monthly CAM = $11,070.00 ÷ 12 = $922.50

  IMPORTANT: If the Original Base Year 2019 ($11.20/SF) were
  used instead (erroneously):

    Expense Increase = $13.40 - $11.20 = $2.20/SF
    Annual CAM = $2.20 × 8,200 = $18,040.00
    Monthly CAM = $18,040.00 ÷ 12 = $1,503.33

  Overcharge per month = $1,503.33 - $922.50 = $580.83
  Overcharge per year  = $580.83 × 12 = $6,969.96
  (Rounded: $6,970.00 annual overcharge)

Section 4.4  CAM Cap.  Tenant's annual share of Operating
Expense increases shall be subject to a cumulative cap of five
percent (5%) per annum compounded from the Amended Base Year.
The cumulative cap operates as follows:

  Year 1 (2022): Max increase = $12.05 × 1.05 = $12.6525
  Year 2 (2023): Max increase = $12.05 × 1.05² = $13.2851
  Year 3 (2024): Max increase = $12.05 × 1.05³ = $13.9494

  Since the actual 2024 expense ($13.40) is below the
  cumulative cap ($13.9494), the cap does not apply in 2024.

                    ARTICLE V — LEASE TYPE

Section 5.1  Modified Gross Lease.  This Lease is a modified
gross lease. Tenant is responsible for its pro-rata share of
Operating Expense increases above the Base Year, as set forth
in Article IV.

                  ARTICLE VI — MISCELLANEOUS

Section 6.1  Audit Rights.  Tenant shall have the right to
audit Landlord's Operating Expense records within one hundred
twenty (120) days following delivery of the annual reconciliation
statement.

Section 6.2  Dispute Resolution.  Any disputes arising under
this Lease shall be resolved through binding arbitration in
Cook County, Illinois.
