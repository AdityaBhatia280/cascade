# 04 — Why Now: Trends Making Data Center Dependency Mapping and Failure Simulation More Urgent

**Scope:** AI rack density and thermal ride-through, liquid cooling, grid and power constraints, retrofits, regulation, and funding. **Researched:** 2026-11-07.

Every claim has a source URL. Related: steady-state cooling physics and limits are in `06-cooling-physics.md`; rack-density figures there (Uptime 2024: average "below 8 kW") are an earlier survey year than §1 below. **How to read this:** Most direct-fetch attempts timed out or returned 403 (Uptime, BusinessWire, DCD). Many figures below therefore come from search-engine snippets of the cited pages and from secondary coverage, not from a full read of the primary document. Each claim notes its confidence: **[P]** = primary source (vendor, regulator or standards body page); **[S]** = secondary (trade press, law firm or aggregator); **[V]** = vendor marketing or a single low-quality source. Verify **[S]**/**[V]** numbers before you put them in a pitch deck.

## Key takeaways

1. **The density gap is widening, and AI racks sit 10–50x above the typical rack.** Uptime's 2026 survey puts the modal rack at 11 kW (up from 9 kW) and the mean at 7.8 kW ([01net/Uptime PR](https://www.01net.it/uptime-institute-16th-annual-2026-global-data-center-survey-deployment-of-high-density-racks-rising-fast-operators-face-continued-recruiting-and-retention-pressures/), [DCK](https://datacenterknowledge.com/ai-data-centers/ai-drives-data-center-uncertainty-in-uptime-s-2026-survey)). By contrast, NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 racks draw about 120 kW, and NVIDIA said in 2025 that Kyber/Rubin Ultra racks would draw about 600 kW in 2027 ([DCD](https://datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nvidias-rubin-ultra-nvl576-rack-expected-to-be-600kw-coming-second-half-of-2027), [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/nvidia-shows-off-rubin-ultra-with-600-000-watt-kyber-racks-and-infrastructure-coming-in-2027)). Most facilities therefore run a mix of legacy and AI loads.
2. **Thermal ride-through has dropped from minutes to seconds.** Stulz describes a 30 kW air-cooled rack giving minutes to respond, while a 130 kW liquid-cooled rack can throttle "in seconds" ([DCD Connect session](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/dcdconnect-live/virginia/2026/session-71930195-ef26-48ba-8f61-97dc393a0ff9/)). ASHRAE TC 9.9 now tells operators to plan failover inside the "minimum server time-to-throttle based on worst-case failure" ([StorageReview on ASHRAE bulletin](https://www.storagereview.com/news/ashrae-tc-9-9-flags-four-ways-direct-to-chip-cooling-loops-go-wrong)). At these timescales a human response is too slow, so dependencies have to be understood before the failure happens.
3. **Liquid cooling adds a new dependency layer and new failure modes.** The chain runs facility water → CDU → technology cooling loop → cold plates. Dell'Oro estimates liquid cooling revenue roughly doubled in 2025 to about $3B and projects about $7B by 2029 ([Dell'Oro](https://www.delloro.com/news/data-center-liquid-cooling-market-to-approach-7-billion-by-2029-as-ai-deployments-accelerate/)). Uptime has said DLC "introduce[s] new failure scenarios" and complicates who is responsible for what ([Uptime Briefing 117](https://uptimeinstitute.com/resources/research-and-reports/resiliency-considerations-with-direct-liquid-cooling)). The Nov 2025 CME outage shows cooling-chain failures cascading to customers: a CyrusOne chiller-plant failure halted CME's markets for more than 10 hours ([Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2025/11/30/cme-outage-financial-markets-data-center-cyrusone-aurora-illinois), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-30/cme-s-data-center-adds-more-cooling-after-outage-cyrusone-says)).
4. **Power chains are getting longer and more interactive.** Grid interconnection waits of 5–7 years are pushing operators to on-site and behind-the-meter generation. Enverus projects about 36% of US data center capacity additions will be served by BTM generation ([Power Engineering/Enverus](https://www.power-eng.com/onsite-power/data-centers-btm-enverus-forecast/)). Texas SB6 lets ERCOT direct large loads to switch to backup generation or curtail during emergencies ([Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/texas-law-gives-grid-operator-power-to-disconnect-data-centers-during-crisis/751587)). NERC issued a rare Level 3 alert in May 2026 after gigawatt-scale data center load-loss events ([Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/nerc-issues-rare-level-3-alert-over-data-center-load-losses/819295/)).
5. **Regulators and investors are both paying attention.** NIS2 classes data centre providers as "essential" and treats more than 1 hour of degraded availability as a reportable significant incident ([Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/de/insights/publications/2024/10/new-eu-cyber-rules-nis2-take-effect-implementing-rules-adopted)). DORA's first critical ICT third-party provider (CTPP) list (Nov 2025) includes data centre providers ([ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/european-supervisory-authorities-designate-critical-ict-third-party-providers), [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/de/blogs/sourcingatmorganlewis/2025/11/dora-eu-regulators-announce-list-of-critical-ict-third-party-providers)). The UK designated data centres as critical national infrastructure (CNI) in Sept 2024 ([Infosecurity](https://infosecurity-magazine.com/news/uk-data-centers-critical-national)). On the capital side: Emerald AI ($150M Series A at a $1.05B valuation, 2026) ([ESG Today](https://esgtoday.com/data-center-power-solutions-startup-emerald-ai-raises-150-million-at-unicorn-valuation)), Phaidra ($50M+ Series B, Oct 2025) ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2025/phaidra-raises-50m-to-help-ai-data-centers-run-smarter-not-just-harder-by-boosting-energy-efficiency/)), and Schneider's $850M purchase of a 75% stake in Motivair ([eeNews Europe](https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/schneider-electric-buys-data-centre-cooling-firm-in-billion-dollar-deal)).

## 1. AI/GPU rack density and shrinking thermal ride-through

### Density trends
- **Uptime 2026 Global Data Center Survey** (16th annual, July 2026, more than 1,600 respondents): modal rack density rose to **11 kW from 9 kW** in 2025. Mean density excluding sites above 30 kW is **7.8 kW, up from 7.5 kW**. The release headline reads "deployment of high-density racks rising fast." [S] [01net (Uptime PR)](https://www.01net.it/uptime-institute-16th-annual-2026-global-data-center-survey-deployment-of-high-density-racks-rising-fast-operators-face-continued-recruiting-and-retention-pressures/), [Data Center Knowledge](https://datacenterknowledge.com/ai-data-centers/ai-drives-data-center-uncertainty-in-uptime-s-2026-survey), primary: [Uptime Keynote Report 209](https://intelligence.uptimeinstitute.com/resource/uptime-institute-global-data-center-survey-2026)
- **Uptime 2025 survey**: more racks in the 10–30 kW range; "few facilities exceed 30 kW." [S] [Uptime 2025 report landing](https://intelligence.uptimeinstitute.com/resource/uptime-institute-global-data-center-survey-2025), [DCK](https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/energy-power-supply/data-center-industry-survey-highlights-cost-ai-and-sustainability-challenges)
- **AFCOM 2026** reports a much higher average of **27 kW, up from 16 kW** (different sample and method; it conflicts with Uptime). [S] via [DCK](https://datacenterknowledge.com/ai-data-centers/ai-drives-data-center-uncertainty-in-uptime-s-2026-survey). Verify against the primary source.
- **AI rack roadmap:** GB200 NVL72 is about **120 kW per rack**. NVIDIA's GTC 2025 statement put Rubin Ultra NVL576 "Kyber" at about **600 kW per rack, 2H 2027**. [S] [DCD](https://datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nvidias-rubin-ultra-nvl576-rack-expected-to-be-600kw-coming-second-half-of-2027), [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/nvidia-shows-off-rubin-ultra-with-600-000-watt-kyber-racks-and-infrastructure-coming-in-2027), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/megawatt-class-ai-server-racks-may-well-become-the-norm-before-2030-as-nvidia-displays-600kw-kyber-rack-design). *Caveat:* one analysis suggests the Kyber design shown at GTC 2026 has half the GPU density, possibly around 300 kW ([glennklockwood.com](https://glennklockwood.com/garden/kyber)). This is unconfirmed.
- JLL's 2026 outlook puts AI training at **about 40–100+ kW per rack**, often requiring liquid cooling. [S] (snippet only; JLL primary not retrieved)

### Ride-through: minutes become seconds
- **Stulz / DCD Connect Virginia 2026:** power ride-through is "largely solved," and thermal ride-through is now the constraint. In their example a **30 kW air-cooled rack gives minutes**, while a **130 kW liquid-cooled rack can throttle or shut down in seconds**. [S] [DCD session page](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/dcdconnect-live/virginia/2026/session-71930195-ef26-48ba-8f61-97dc393a0ff9/)
- **ASHRAE TC 9.9** (bulletin "TCS Coolant Integrity and System Readiness Best Practices," companion to the 2024 *Liquid Cooling Resiliency Guidance for Cold Plate Deployments*): operators should add thermal inertia and active redundancy, and plan load migration to "work within the timeframe of the minimum server time-to-throttle based on worst-case failure of the resilient design." [S] [StorageReview](https://www.storagereview.com/news/ashrae-tc-9-9-flags-four-ways-direct-to-chip-cooling-loops-go-wrong)
- **ASHRAE research project 1972-TRP**, "Data Center Direct-to-Chip Liquid Cooling Resiliency – Failure Modes and IT Throttling Impacts," shows the standards body still treats these failure modes as under-characterized. [P] [ASHRAE RFP PDF](https://www.ashrae.org/file%20library/technical%20resources/research/research%20project%20bidding/1972-rfp.pdf)
- **Direct-to-chip ride-through is often quoted at under 10 s at full load.** [V] [Introl](https://introl.com/blog/liquid-cooling-gpu-data-centers-50kw-thermal-limits-guide), [progressiverobot](https://www.progressiverobot.com/?p=247475). A Tom's Hardware report cites Austin Reliability Labs, where single-phase leaks caused rack failure in just under 10 s. [S] [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/liquid-cooling/the-hidden-danger-lurking-in-your-server-infrastructure-its-own-cooling-system)
- **Honeywell** sells a "Thermal Response Module" that bridges cooling during chiller restarts. Its pitch is that liquid loops lack the thermal inertia air-cooled systems had. This is a vendor product, but it shows a market forming around the problem. [V] [Honeywell](https://www.honeywellaerospace.com/us/en/products-and-services/products/control-systems/air-and-thermal-systems/high-density-cooling/thermal-response-module)
- **Uptime's earlier guidance** recommended continuous cooling above 4 kW per rack at any tier, and counts the chiller restart time against the UPS ride-through window. [V] (2017 paper, secondary copy) [Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/636199118/Continuous-Cooling)

**What this means for Cascade:** At 100+ kW per rack, the gap between "pump trips" and "GPUs throttle" is too short for anyone to look things up in a runbook. Operators need to know ahead of time which CDU, pump, chiller or power feed each GPU hall depends on, and what an outage there costs. That pre-computed answer is what Cascade sells.

## 2. Liquid cooling adoption: new chains, new failure modes

### Adoption and market
- **Dell'Oro (early 2026):** liquid cooling roughly **doubled in 2025 to close to $3B** and is projected at **about $7B by 2029**. Single-phase DLC stays dominant through the decade. [P] [Dell'Oro](https://www.delloro.com/news/data-center-liquid-cooling-market-to-approach-7-billion-by-2029-as-ai-deployments-accelerate/). DLC revenue grew 156% Y/Y in 2Q 2025. [S] [Fierce Network](https://www.fierce-network.com/cloud/theres-no-stopping-liquid-cooling-train)
- **Uptime 2025 cooling survey:** about **22%** of organisations use DLC (figure from a search snippet; primary crosstabs at [Uptime](https://intelligence.uptimeinstitute.com/resource/2025-cooling-systems-survey-results-and-crosstab-files)). **AFCOM 2025:** 19% use liquid cooling today and 36% plan to within 1–2 years. [V] via [Badger Meter](https://www.badgermeter.com/blog/data-center-liquid-cooling-strategies/)

### New dependency chains and failure modes
- **The CDU is now the boundary between the facility water system (FWS) and the technology cooling system (TCS).** ASHRAE TC 9.9 lists coolant-driven failure paths: altered pressure drop, shifted pump operating point, degraded heat-exchanger effectiveness, and CDU control instability. It also covers air entrainment (venting, separators) and warns against mixing coolants. [S] [StorageReview](https://www.storagereview.com/news/ashrae-tc-9-9-flags-four-ways-direct-to-chip-cooling-loops-go-wrong)
- **Uptime Briefing Report 117 (Nov 2023):** DLC "introduce[s] new failure scenarios" and complicates the allocation of responsibilities between facility and IT. [P, summary only] [Uptime](https://uptimeinstitute.com/resources/research-and-reports/resiliency-considerations-with-direct-liquid-cooling)
- Academic testing of an in-row liquid-to-liquid CDU covered total and partial primary flow loss, pump failures and CDU failover. [P] [UT Arlington dissertation](https://mavmatrix.uta.edu/mechaerospace_dissertations/434)
- **Mitigations operators use:** N+1 pumps, dual-CDU feeds, tested automatic failover within seconds, and protection logic in the CDU or rack rather than human response. [V] [progressiverobot](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2022/06/30/liquid-cooling-failures-ai-data-centers/), [AI Certs](https://www.aicerts.ai/news/?p=22182). Schneider's White Paper 133 lists six common liquid cooling architectures (CDU type × heat-rejection method), which means there is no single standard topology to model. [P] [Schneider PR](https://www.se.com/ie/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/schneider-electric-publishes-new-white-paper-on-liquid-cooling-for-ai-data-centres-66717048fdbb685dd102bc29)

### Case: a cooling-chain failure cascading to customers (illustrative, not outage stats)
- On **27–28 Nov 2025** a chiller-plant failure at CyrusOne CHI1 (Aurora, IL) took out several cooling units. CME's Globex markets were halted for more than 10 hours. CyrusOne then added backup cooling. [S] [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2025/11/30/cme-outage-financial-markets-data-center-cyrusone-aurora-illinois), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-30/cme-s-data-center-adds-more-cooling-after-outage-cyrusone-says), [Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/midwest/2025/12/01/849319.htm). This is one shared cooling plant feeding one high-value tenant, the dependency-to-SLA-dollar mapping Cascade models.

## 3. Grid and power constraints: more complex power chains

- **Interconnection waits:** 5–7 years in constrained markets. Examples cited include 84 months in Columbus, Ohio and about 7 years for Dominion customers. [S/V] [Enverus](https://www.enverus.com/blog/time-to-power-how-data-center-developers-can-fast-track-energization-in-a-constrained-grid/), [Avanza Energy](https://avanzaenergy.substack.com/p/data-centers-are-killing-the-grid)
- **Behind-the-meter (BTM) shift:** Enverus projects that **about 36% of US data center capacity additions** will be served by BTM generation. One report counts at least 46 data centers pursuing about 56 GW of on-site gas. [S] [Power Engineering](https://www.power-eng.com/onsite-power/data-centers-btm-enverus-forecast/), [Modo Energy](https://modoenergy.com/research/bring-your-own-power-data-centers-explainer). Modo also describes "bridge power": on-site generation first, grid connection later. Over its lifetime, such a site's power topology changes.
- **Texas SB6 (signed June 2025):** large loads must disclose backup generation able to cover at least 50% of demand. In emergencies ERCOT may direct them to deploy backup or curtail. New large loads (interconnected after 31 Dec 2025) must support remote disconnect. There is also a voluntary demand-response program for loads of 75 MW or more. PUCT rulemaking continues through 2026. [S] [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/texas-law-gives-grid-operator-power-to-disconnect-data-centers-during-crisis/751587), [McGuireWoods](https://www.mcguirewoods.com/client-resources/alerts/2025/7/texas-senate-bill-6-significantly-expands-regulatory-oversight-over-large-loads-in-ercot/), [Data Center Frontier](https://www.datacenterfrontier.com/energy/article/55298872/texas-senate-bill-6-a-bellwether-on-how-states-may-approach-data-center-energy-use)
- **NERC:** in July 2024 about **1,500 MW** of data center load transferred to backup power after a 230 kV fault, an event where data center protection logic interacted with grid disturbances. [P] [NERC Incident Review](https://nerc.com/pa/rrm/ea/Documents/Incident_Review_Large_Load_Loss.pdf), [APPA](https://www.publicpower.org/periodical/article/nerc-incident-review-examines-risks-challenges-tied-integration-large-loads). On **4 May 2026** NERC issued a **Level 3 "Essential Actions" alert**, only the third in its history, covering modeling, commissioning, protection and control of "computational load." [S] [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/nerc-issues-rare-level-3-alert-over-data-center-load-losses/819295/), [Data Center Frontier](https://www.datacenterfrontier.com/energy/article/55376679/why-nerc-now-sees-ai-data-centers-as-grid-actors), [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/ja/blogs/datacenterbytes/2026/05/nerc-alert-signals-growing-reliability-focus-on-data-centers). Sources conflict on whether the actions are mandatory.
- **FERC co-location order (18 Dec 2025):** directs PJM to create transmission service options for co-located loads, including interim non-firm service, and revisits behind-the-meter generation (BTMG) rules. [S] [Jones Day](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/12/ferc-issues-anticipated-order-on-data-center-colocation-in-pjm-region), [Foley](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2025/12/ferc-opens-new-paths-for-co-located-loads-in-pjm-what-data-center-and-power-gene/)
- **Grid flexibility / demand response:** Emerald AI's Conductor adjusts data center consumption under grid stress. Its first deployment is the 96 MW NVIDIA Aurora facility in Manassas, VA. [S] [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nvidia-backed-emerald-ai-raises-245m-to-turn-data-centers-into-grid-assets/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/03/31/emerald-ai-nvidia-fast-pass-data-center-grid-connects/)
- **New in-building power architectures:** NVIDIA's 800 VDC roadmap adds an 800 VDC power rack for existing AC facilities (2H 2026), a row power center of up to 2 MW per row (2027), and solid-state-transformer designs. An OCP LVDC SST spec v0.3 from NVIDIA, Google and Microsoft is reported for July 2026. [P] [NVIDIA blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/800-vdc-power-architecture-ai-factory), [NVIDIA developer blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-800-v-hvdc-architecture-will-power-the-next-generation-of-ai-factories/). Hybrid AC/DC buildings mean even more heterogeneous power chains.
- **Demand context:** the IEA projects global data center electricity demand will more than double to **about 945 TWh by 2030** (from about 415 TWh in 2024), with AI-optimised DC demand more than quadrupling. [S] [RCR Wireless on IEA](https://rcrwireless.com/20250411/rcr-techtrends/ai-electricity-dcs-2030-iea)

**What this means for Cascade:** utility feed, on-site gas or fuel cells, batteries, UPS, gensets, grid-directed curtailment and 800 VDC conversion each add nodes and failure modes. "What breaks if X goes offline" is no longer readable from a simple one-line diagram.

## 4. Construction and retrofit pace: legacy and new mixed together

- **STL Partners (May 2026):** liquid-cooling retrofits cost about **$2M/MW vs. $11M+/MW greenfield** (about 80% cheaper). There is a threshold effect around 40 kW per rack, and roadmaps head toward 200 kW+. Operators are "under growing pressure to retrofit." [S] [STL Partners](https://stlpartners.com/?p=522064), [Data Centre Review](https://datacentrereview.com/2026/06/liquid-cooling-retrofits-could-cut-ai-data-centre-upgrade-costs-by-around-80/)
- **Vertiv colocation case (2026):** facilities built for 5 kW racks are now getting 30–60 kW AI requests and are filling the gap with modular liquid cooling. [V] [Vertiv](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/insights/articles/educational-articles/vertiv-cooling-innovation-day-2026-lessons-from-a-live-colocation-deployment/?Advocacy-Region=na)
- **Uptime:** "ease of retrofit" is the top factor in evaluating liquid cooling, cited by 46% (figure via vendor blog). [V] [Badger Meter](https://www.badgermeter.com/blog/data-center-liquid-cooling-strategies/). Integration complexity (47%) ranks ahead of cost (41%) as a barrier. [V] same source.
- **Data Center Knowledge:** older facilities may lack mechanical-plant capacity for liquid retrofits. [S] [DCK](https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/cooling/liquid-cooling-retrofits-hard-but-sometimes-worth-it). **Tate:** congested underfloor space and live-site leak risk. [V] [Tate](https://www.tateglobal.com/emea/insights-resources/knowledge-hub/retrofitting-liquid-cooling-for-ai-data-centers/)
- NVIDIA's 800 VDC "power rack" is explicitly designed to drop into *existing AC facilities* [P] ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/800-vdc-power-architecture-ai-factory)). Hybrid power retrofits are planned, not hypothetical.

**What this means for Cascade:** a retrofit hall shares legacy chillers and switchgear with bolt-on CDUs and new busways. That mixed estate is where as-built documentation goes stale. *Inference, not sourced:* I found no survey that quantifies "undocumented dependencies" in retrofits.

## 5. Regulation and reporting

### EU
- **Energy Efficiency Directive (EED, recast 2023/1791), Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1364:** in force 6 June 2024. Data centres of **500 kW or more** of installed IT power report KPIs to the EU database. The first report was due **15 Sept 2024**, then every **15 May** after that. [P] [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2024/1364/oj), [European Commission](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-targets-directive-and-rules/energy-efficiency-directive/energy-performance-data-centres_en), [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-fr/insights/publications/2024/09/new-data-centre-sustainability-reporting-obligations-introduced-through-eu-20241364-regulation)
- **EU data centre rating/label scheme:** a draft delegated regulation was consulted on in Mar–Apr 2026. Noerr reports a Commission proposal on 21 Sept 2026, and Philip Lee advises preparing for labels from **Aug 2027**. It is still a draft. [P/S] [Draft PDF](https://www.thegreenwebfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/EU.datacentre.labelling.scheme-draft-delegated-regulation.Ares_.2026.3247482pdf.pdf), [Noerr](https://www.noerr.com/en/insights/eu-fuhrt-gemeinsames-bewertungssystem-und-nachhaltigkeitskennzeichnung-fur-rechenzentren-ein), [DLA Piper 2026](https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/blogs/environment-health-safety-and-product-compliance/2026/eu-commission-proposes-new-data-centre-sustainability-rating-label), [Philip Lee](https://www.philiplee.ie/?p=394333)
- **NIS2:** data centre service providers are "essential" entities. The Implementing Regulation (Oct 2024) defines a **significant incident** for data centres as a service being fully unavailable, or **availability limited for more than 1 hour**. Reporting runs on a 24 h early warning, a 72 h notification and a 1-month final report. [S] [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/de/insights/publications/2024/10/new-eu-cyber-rules-nis2-take-effect-implementing-rules-adopted), [openstatus](https://www.openstatus.dev/guides/nis2-incident-reporting-requirements). *Relevance:* operators need to know fast which services and customers a facility event degrades.
- **DORA:** on **18 Nov 2025** the ESAs designated the first list of critical ICT third-party providers (CTPPs), reportedly 19, spanning hyperscale cloud, **data centre**, infrastructure and network providers. These come under direct ESA oversight, and the list is updated annually. [P] [ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/european-supervisory-authorities-designate-critical-ict-third-party-providers), [EBA](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/european-supervisory-authorities-designate-critical-ict-third-party-providers-under-digital%5C); [S] [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/de/blogs/sourcingatmorganlewis/2025/11/dora-eu-regulators-announce-list-of-critical-ict-third-party-providers). DORA also requires financial entities to map ICT third-party dependencies through their registers of information. Colos serving banks will face more questions about concentration and resilience from those customers. (The "will face" part is my inference.)
- **CER Directive (Critical Entities Resilience):** I could not find a source on how it applies to data centres. See Gaps.

### UK
- Data centres were **designated critical national infrastructure** on 12 Sept 2024, the first new CNI sector since 2015. [S] [Infosecurity](https://infosecurity-magazine.com/news/uk-data-centers-critical-national), [UKAuthority](https://www.ukauthority.com/articles/data-centres-declared-part-of-uk-critical-national-infrastructure)
- The **Cyber Security and Resilience (NIS) Bill** was introduced 12 Nov 2025. Data centres of **1 MW or more** (enterprise sites at 10 MW or more) become operators of essential services. It is not yet law.
