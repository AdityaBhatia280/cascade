7. SERVICE LEVELS
7.1 Availability Commitment. Provider will maintain Power Availability of 99.99% per calendar month, excluding Scheduled Maintenance (Section 9).
7.2 Power Outage. A Power Outage occurs when a Licensed Cabinet loses power on all contracted feeds.
    For redundant (A+B or 2N) service, loss of a single feed is not a Power Outage.
7.3 Exclusions. No credit is owed where (a) the Customer is not connected to all contracted feeds,
    (b) Customer load on a redundant pair exceeds 80% of a single circuit rating, or (c) the event
    occurs during Scheduled Maintenance.

8. SERVICE CREDITS
8.1 Credits are calculated on the Monthly Recurring Charge (MRC) of the affected Licensed Cabinets:
      Cumulative Power Outage per month      Credit
      10 - 43 minutes                         5% of MRC
      43 - 240 minutes                        10% of MRC
      240 - 720 minutes                       25% of MRC
      > 720 minutes                           50% of MRC
8.2 Total credits in any month shall not exceed 50% of the MRC. Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy
    except as provided in Section 8.3 (chronic failure termination right).

9. SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE
9.1 Provider will give at least ten (10) business days written notice of Scheduled Maintenance.
9.2 Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to keep at least one contracted feed energized
    during Scheduled Maintenance for redundant service.
