Section 4.1 Base Rent. $32.00 per rentable square foot per year, increasing 3.0% on each anniversary.

Section 5.2 Operating Expenses. Tenant pays its pro rata share of Operating Expenses in excess of
Operating Expenses for the Base Year. Base Year means calendar year 2019.

Section 5.4 Pro Rata Share. Tenant's Pro Rata Share is the rentable area of the Premises divided by the
TOTAL rentable area of the Building (120,000 sf), regardless of occupancy.
